# KYGR Solutions

The public marketing site and visitor-facing language for KYGR Solutions, Kyle
Greer's one-person software and automation practice.

## Visitor actions

**Consult call**:
A free 30-minute call a visitor books directly on Kyle's calendar.
_Avoid_: Tech audit, free consult, discovery call

**Message**:
A written enquiry a visitor sends through the contact form.
_Avoid_: Email (when meaning the form), consult request

**Lead**:
A visitor who has sent a Message. Clicking to book a Consult call, call,
email, or open the contact form to write a Message shows intent but is not,
by itself, a Lead.
_Avoid_: Conversion (for clicks), prospect

**Intent click**:
A click on a Consult call, phone, email, or "Send me a message" link. It is
counted separately from Leads and never counts as one.
_Avoid_: Conversion, lead (for a click)

## Social proof

**Google review**:
A review a client left on the KYGR Solutions Google Business Profile, shown on
the site verbatim (optionally as an excerpt of their own words).
_Avoid_: Testimonial (when the source is Google), quote
